Sliding window (NEW pattern — first one). Expand the right edge, shrink the left edge past any duplicate, track the max window size.

🧠 MY APPROACH
Solved in ~10 minutes, first attempt, no hints. I had seen this problem before, so this measured recall + execution, not fresh derivation — logged honestly. Code: for each r, mp[s[r]]++; while duplicate, mp[s[l++]]--; ans = max(ans, r-l+1). Correct on all cases including empty string.
Complexity analysis unprompted and correct: O(n) time — each character enters and leaves the window once (amortized analysis, the exact idea from my Day 1 theory deep-dive, now in my own code); O(1) space — the map holds at most one entry per distinct char and the charset is bounded (~256), so constant regardless of n. ⏱️ RESULT
Solved correctly in ~10 min — my fastest medium yet. The invariant behind it (taught post-solve): after the shrink step, [l, r] is always the longest duplicate-free substring ending at r, because any valid substring ending at r cannot contain the older duplicate — so l must jump past it. Max over every r covers every candidate answer.
